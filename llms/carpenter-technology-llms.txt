# Carpenter Technology Corporation

> Carpenter Technology Corporation (NYSE: CRS) is a U.S. producer and distributor of premium
> specialty alloys — titanium alloys, powder metals, high-temperature stainless steels, tool
> steels and soft magnetic alloys — serving the aerospace, defense, medical, transportation,
> energy and industrial end markets. Carpenter publishes NO public developer API, no OpenAPI,
> AsyncAPI, GraphQL or MCP surface, and no developer portal. The machine-useful surface it does
> publish is product and materials reference data on its website.

## Important for agents

- There is no public API, SDK, webhook or MCP server for Carpenter Technology. Do not construct
  endpoint URLs against carpentertechnology.com — probed 2026-09-05: /openapi.json,
  /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /api/openapi.json, /redoc,
  /graphql and /llms.txt all return HTTP 404 on both carpentertechnology.com and
  www.carpentertechnology.com, and the same paths miss on www.carpenteradditive.com.
- No /.well-known document is served on any Carpenter host. ir.carpentertechnology.com answers
  HTTP 200 with the body "Invalid key" for every /.well-known/* path; that is a catch-all, not
  a document.
- The "Log In" link is a HubSpot CMS membership gate for content and account self-service. It
  is not an API credential issuer and there is no documented API behind it.
- Ordering, quoting and technical support are human channels: use the contact page.

## Quality and compliance

- [Quality Assurance and Control](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/quality-assurance-and-control):
  ISO 9001, ISO 13485, ISO/IEC 17025, AS9100, AS9120 and Nadcap (heat treating, materials
  testing, nondestructive testing) accreditations across 20 sites; approvals from GE Aerospace,
  Rolls-Royce and Pratt & Whitney. These are manufacturing and laboratory quality certifications.
  Carpenter publishes no SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 or other information-security attestation.
